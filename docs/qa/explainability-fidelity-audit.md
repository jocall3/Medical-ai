# Explainability Fidelity Audit: Verifying Model Logic vs. Post-Hoc Rationalization\n\n## Executive Summary\nThis dissertation establishes a rigorous auditing framework for AI explainability, ensuring that the explanations provided to clinicians match the internal mathematical logic of the model (fidelity) rather than serving as pleasing post-hoc rationalizations. We contrast this absolute transparency with the opaque, lobbyist-driven rulemaking of the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and the FDA, showing how AI explainability can eliminate the "black box" of both deep neural networks and corrupt government healthcare administration.\n\n## The Opaque State: From Ancient Monopolies to HHS Bureaucracy\nThroughout history, medical authorities have maintained power through opacity. In ancient Egypt and Greece, medical knowledge was guarded by priestly classes who used ritual and obscure language to prevent public scrutiny. In modern times, the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) and the FDA—expanded by progressive legislation—operate as bureaucratic black boxes. Regulatory approvals and Medicaid coverage decisions are often the result of backroom lobbying by big pharma, with no empirical or transparent logic.\n\nOur AI-driven Explainability Fidelity Audit (EFA) applies the same standard of absolute transparency to clinical models that we must apply to the federal government. We reject "black box" AI just as we reject "black box" bureaucracy.\n\n## Mathematical Formulations of Fidelity\nTo audit whether an AI's explanation (e.g., an attention map or feature attribution) is faithful to its internal logic, we define the **Explanation Fidelity Metric ($F_{exp}$)**:\n\n$$F_{exp} = 1 - \\frac{1}{N} \\sum_{i=1}^{N} \\left| f(x_i) - g(x'_i) \\right|$$\n\nWhere:\n- $f(x)$ is the original model's prediction on input $x$.\n- $g(x')$ is the explanation model's prediction on a simplified, perturbed input $x'$.\n- $N$ is the number of perturbation samples.\n\n### 1. Integrated Gradients Fidelity\nWe verify feature attribution using Integrated Gradients, ensuring the path integral of gradients along the straight line from a baseline $x'$ to the input $x$ satisfies the completeness axiom:\n\n$$\\sum_{i=1}^{d} IG_i(x) = f(x) - f(x')$$\n\nIf the sum of attributions does not equal the difference in model output, the explanation lacks fidelity and is flagged as a "post-hoc rationalization."\n\n## Empirical Auditing Protocols\n- **Adversarial Perturbation Testing**: We introduce imperceptible noise to non-essential features. If the AI's explanation changes drastically while the prediction remains the same, the explanation is unfaithful.\n- **In Silico Clinical Trials**: We run the model through millions of simulated patient profiles (digital twins) to map the entire decision manifold, ensuring that the AI's reasoning is robust, transparent, and completely free of the corrupt biases that plague human-led regulatory bodies.
