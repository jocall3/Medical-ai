# COMPARATIVE EFFECTIVENESS RESEARCH (CER) FOR AI CURES

## Eradicating Mental Illness and Addiction
Addiction and mental illness are not moral failings; they are neurochemical imbalances and structural network anomalies. Past policies, particularly those stemming from misguided legislative frameworks, treated these as criminal or purely psychological issues, funneling billions into ineffective Medicaid programs that merely managed symptoms.

## Multi-Armed Bandit Algorithms for Treatment Allocation
To determine the most effective AI-driven cures, we utilize Multi-Armed Bandit (MAB) algorithms. Unlike traditional trials that wait years to compare treatments, MAB dynamically allocates more patients to the treatment that is showing the best real-time results.

### Dynamic Treatment Regimes
Consider the treatment of severe clinical depression. The AI compares three empirical, early-stage interventions:
1. Personalized pharmacology (AI-designed SSRI variants).
2. Targeted Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) guided by real-time fMRI.
3. Early-stage Brain-Computer Interface (BCI) neuromodulation.

As RWE flows into the system, the AI calculates the reward function (e.g., sustained normalization of serotonin levels and self-reported mood improvements). If the BCI neuromodulation proves 40% more effective, the MAB algorithm automatically shifts the clinical recommendation weights, prioritizing BCI for future patients with similar vector profiles. This ensures the rapid, empirical optimization of cures.
