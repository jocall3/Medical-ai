# Virtual Ward Stress Testing: Operational AI Integration

## 1. Objective
To simulate the operational impact of AI-driven cures on the healthcare infrastructure to prevent the 'Success Paradox'—where a cure is so effective it crashes the hospital system due to sudden patient surges.

## 2. Simulation Parameters

### 2.1 Resource Allocation Modeling
We model the hospital as a queuing system (M/M/c queue). We simulate the transition of patients from 'Chronic Care' to 'Acute Cure' and measure the impact on:
- **ICU Bed Occupancy**: Predicting the surge in patients requiring short-term intensive monitoring during the cure process.
- **Staffing Ratios**: Calculating the required nurse-to-patient ratio for AI-managed wards.
- **Supply Chain**: Simulating the demand for the cure's raw materials.

### 2.2 Bottleneck Identification
Using Monte Carlo simulations, we identify the 'Breaking Point' of the hospital—the exact number of patients per hour that leads to a collapse in care quality.

## 3. Strategic Recommendation
To avoid the bottlenecks created by the current bloated Medicaid system, we propose 'Decentralized Cure Centers'—AI-managed clinics that bypass the traditional hospital bureaucracy, delivering cures directly to the citizen.
