# AUTOMATED ADVERSE EVENT REPORTING

## Dismantling Archaic Systems
The current systems for reporting adverse medical events (such as VAERS or FAERS) are antiquated, relying on voluntary, manual data entry by overworked healthcare professionals. This has led to massive underreporting and delayed responses to dangerous drugs. This failure is a direct result of legislative frameworks that prioritized liability protection over patient safety.

## AI-Driven Telemetry and Reporting
We propose a fully automated, AI-driven reporting system that interfaces directly with regulatory bodies.

### Natural Language Processing (NLP) Extraction
The AI continuously scans physician notes, nursing logs, and patient communications using advanced Transformer models (e.g., ClinicalBERT). It is trained to identify the semantic signatures of adverse events, even when not explicitly stated.

### IoT Vitals Integration
For patients utilizing advanced therapeutics (e.g., early-stage neural implants for addiction control), the AI monitors IoT telemetry. A sudden spike in cortisol or abnormal cardiac rhythms immediately following a therapeutic intervention is flagged.

### Automated Regulatory Submission
Upon detecting an adverse event, the system automatically compiles a comprehensive dossier—including patient history, genomic profile, the specific AI intervention, and the exact timestamp of the anomaly—and submits it via secure API to the newly restructured, AI-augmented regulatory oversight committee. This ensures 100% compliance and zero delay, providing the empirical data necessary to protect the American public.
