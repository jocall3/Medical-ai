# Autonomous Robotic Interventions in Fetal Surgery

## Overview
Fetal surgery presents immense challenges due to the delicate nature of the patient and the dynamic environment of the womb. This document details the theoretical application of the Robotic Surgery Vision (RSV) system for autonomous, sub-millimeter corrections of congenital defects such as myelomeningocele (spina bifida).

## Robotic Surgery Vision (RSV) System
### Real-Time 3D Mapping
The RSV utilizes advanced ultrasound and MRI fusion to create a real-time, high-fidelity 3D map of the fetus and the uterine environment, adjusting instantaneously for fetal movement.

### Autonomous Path Planning
Machine learning algorithms calculate the optimal surgical approach, minimizing tissue trauma. The AI controls micro-manipulators with precision exceeding human capability, performing complex suturing and tissue grafting in utero.

## Post-Operative AI Monitoring
Following intervention, the AI system continuously monitors fetal vitals and uterine stability to prevent premature labor and ensure graft integration.
