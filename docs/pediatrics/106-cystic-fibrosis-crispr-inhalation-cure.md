# AI-Designed CRISPR Delivery Systems for Cystic Fibrosis

## Overview
Cystic Fibrosis (CF) is caused by mutations in the CFTR gene. This document details the theoretical development of an AI-optimized, inhaled gene-editing therapy designed to correct the mutation directly in the lung epithelium.

## Lipid Nanoparticle (LNP) Optimization
### AI-Driven Molecular Design
Machine learning models simulate millions of lipid formulations to identify the optimal LNP structure that can penetrate the thick mucus layer characteristic of CF lungs without triggering an immune response.

### CRISPR-Cas9 Payload
The AI designs highly specific guide RNAs to target the exact CFTR mutation (e.g., F508del) of the patient. The inhaled LNP delivers the CRISPR machinery to the basal cells of the airway, aiming for a permanent genetic correction and restoration of normal mucosal function.
