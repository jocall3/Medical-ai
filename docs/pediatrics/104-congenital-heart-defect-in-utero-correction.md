# AI-Guided 3D Bioprinting for Congenital Heart Defects

## Overview
Severe congenital heart defects, such as hypoplastic left heart syndrome, require immediate intervention upon birth. This protocol outlines a theoretical approach using AI-guided 3D bioprinting to repair cardiac structures in utero.

## AI Modeling of Cardiac Structures
### Predictive Morphogenesis
The AI analyzes early fetal echocardiograms to predict the trajectory of cardiac malformations. It then designs patient-specific biological patches or valve structures required for correction.

### In Situ Bioprinting
Using a minimally invasive robotic catheter, the AI directs the deposition of autologous stem-cell-derived bio-ink directly onto the fetal heart. The AI monitors the integration of the printed tissue with the developing myocardium, ensuring functional adaptation before birth.
