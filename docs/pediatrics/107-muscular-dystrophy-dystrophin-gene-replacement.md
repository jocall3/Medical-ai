# Vector Optimization for Dystrophin Gene Replacement

## Overview
Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) requires the systemic delivery of a functional dystrophin gene. This document outlines how AI can optimize viral vectors for safe, whole-body muscle transduction.

## AI-Engineered Viral Vectors
### Non-Immunogenic Capsid Design
Using deep learning models of the human immune system, the AI engineers novel adeno-associated virus (AAV) capsids that evade neutralizing antibodies, allowing for safe, repeated dosing if necessary.

### Micro-Dystrophin Optimization
Due to the large size of the dystrophin gene, the AI designs an optimized "micro-dystrophin" construct that retains maximum structural functionality while fitting within the viral payload capacity, ensuring robust expression in cardiac and skeletal muscle.
