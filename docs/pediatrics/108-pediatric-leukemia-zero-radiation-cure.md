# Precision Oncology: AI-Guided CAR-T Therapies

## Overview
To eliminate the long-term developmental toxicities associated with pediatric radiation and broad-spectrum chemotherapy, this document proposes a theoretical framework for relying entirely on AI-guided cellular immunotherapies for pediatric leukemia.

## AI in Cell Therapy Engineering
### Antigen Discovery
The AI analyzes the genomic and proteomic profile of the patient's specific leukemia cells to identify unique, highly specific surface antigens, minimizing off-target effects on healthy tissue.

### CAR-T and CAR-NK Optimization
The AI designs the Chimeric Antigen Receptors (CARs) to maximize binding affinity and T-cell persistence. Furthermore, the AI models the patient's immune system to predict and mitigate Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS), ensuring a safe and highly effective treatment profile.
