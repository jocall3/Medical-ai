# Advanced Vitals Monitoring for SIDS Prevention

## Overview
Sudden Infant Death Syndrome (SIDS) remains a critical pediatric concern. This document explores the theoretical deployment of advanced, non-contact sensor arrays powered by AI anomaly detection to monitor infant vitals and prevent respiratory failure.

## Non-Contact Sensor Arrays
### High-Fidelity Biometric Tracking
Utilizing ultra-wideband radar and advanced optical sensors, the system monitors heart rate, respiratory rate, and micro-movements without physical contact, ensuring the infant's sleep is undisturbed.

### AI Anomaly Detection
A dedicated neural network processes the biometric data streams in real-time. By establishing a personalized baseline for the infant, the AI can detect the earliest signs of apnea or bradycardia.

## Automated Intervention Protocols
Upon detecting a critical anomaly, the system initiates a tiered response, ranging from auditory and kinetic stimulation to alert caregivers, aiming to restore normal respiratory function instantly.
