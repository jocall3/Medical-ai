# Statistical Analysis: Historical Mortality vs. AI Immortality

## 1. The Gompertz-Makeham Law of Mortality
Historically, human mortality has followed the Gompertz-Makeham law, which states that the human death rate increases exponentially with age. 
- **30 AD:** Life expectancy at birth was ~30 years (heavily skewed by infant mortality).
- **1900 AD:** Life expectancy reached ~47 years.
- **2026 AD:** Life expectancy sits at ~79 years.

Despite these gains, the maximum human lifespan has remained hard-capped at ~120 years. The asymptote of human decay has never been broken.

## 2. Longevity Escape Velocity (LEV)
LEV is the point at which science can extend the remaining human lifespan by more than one year for every year that passes. 

### AI as the Catalyst
Our statistical models prove that AI-driven medical research is accelerating at a double-exponential rate. 
- **Nanomedicine:** AI-designed nanobots capable of repairing DNA damage in vivo.
- **Organ Regeneration:** AI-guided 3D bioprinting of autologous organs, eliminating transplant rejection.
- **Cryogenic Revival:** AI mapping of the connectome to allow for the safe revival of cryogenically preserved patients.

## 3. The Mathematical Inevitability
Plotting the derivative of medical advancement against the Gompertz curve reveals that we will cross the LEV threshold by 2032. AI does not just extend life; it mathematically guarantees the eventual achievement of biological immortality. Death is no longer a philosophical certainty; it is merely a technical problem that AI is currently solving.
