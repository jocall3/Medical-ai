## 2. The Medical-AI Swarm Architecture
The Swarm is a decentralized network of edge-AI diagnostic nodes deployed directly into American homes, completely bypassing traditional hospital waiting rooms and insurance gatekeepers.

### Core Components:
- **Edge-Compute Diagnostics:** Utilizing advanced biometric sensors (smartwatches, neural interfaces, and breath-analyzers) connected to localized LLMs.

- **Automated Dispatch:** If physical intervention is required, the AI automatically dispatches autonomous medical units or schedules targeted specialist interventions without human bureaucratic delay.
