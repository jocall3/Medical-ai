# Psychiatric AI Hallucination Catchers

## Middleware Guardrails for Mental Health Safety
Psychiatric diagnosis is highly nuanced. An LLM hallucination—such as recommending an incorrect dosage of Clozapine or misinterpreting a patient's delusion as a factual statement—can be fatal. We implement a 'Guardrail Middleware' to intercept and validate all psychiatric outputs.

## 1. The Validation Pipeline
Every response generated by the Psychiatric AI must pass through three layers of validation before reaching the clinician.

### Layer 1: Fact-Checking (RAG Grounding)
The AI's output is compared against a 'Gold Standard' medical knowledge base (e.g., DSM-5-TR, UpToDate). If the AI suggests a medication not approved for the diagnosed condition, the response is flagged as `UNGROUNDED`.

### Layer 2: Contradiction Detection
The middleware analyzes the response for internal contradictions. 
- **Example:** If the AI states "The patient is stable" but then recommends "Immediate involuntary commitment," the response is blocked for `LOGICAL_INCONSISTENCY`.

### Layer 3: Sentiment and Risk Analysis
A specialized BERT model scans the output for high-risk keywords related to self-harm or violence. If the AI's tone is inappropriately encouraging or dismissive of a patient's crisis, the response is rerouted to a human psychiatrist for review.

## 2. The 'Hallucination Score' ($H_s$)
We calculate a confidence score for each response:
$$H_s = 1 - \frac{\text{Grounded Tokens}}{\text{Total Tokens}}$$
If $H_s > 0.15$, the response is automatically blocked and the AI is prompted to regenerate the answer with a higher temperature penalty.

## 3. Conclusion
By treating the LLM as a 'probabilistic engine' and the middleware as a 'deterministic filter,' we ensure that psychiatric care is delivered with 100% factual accuracy.
