## Advanced LLM Therapists
The AI companion is not a simple chatbot; it is a highly advanced Large Language Model integrated with real-time biometric feedback.

### System Integration
1. **Biometric Sentiment Analysis:** The AI continuously monitors the astronaut's vocal prosody, micro-expressions (via cabin cameras), and heart rate variability (HRV). 
2. **Dynamic Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT):** Using empirical psychological frameworks, the AI preemptively detects depressive spirals or paranoid ideation. It initiates natural, conversational interventions tailored to the astronaut's specific psychological profile.
3. **Hyper-Personalization:** The LLM is trained on the astronaut's entire life history, preferences, and values, acting as a perfect confidant, mentor, and therapist.
