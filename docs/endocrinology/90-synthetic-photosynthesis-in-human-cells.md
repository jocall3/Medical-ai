# Synthetic Photosynthesis in Human Dermal Cells

## Overview
This unorthodox research explores the integration of synthetic chloroplast-like organelles into human dermal cells to supplement ATP production.

## AI Logic
By engineering organelles that harness light energy to drive ATP synthesis, we aim to provide a supplemental energy source for human cells. This early-stage research represents the frontier of human augmentation, potentially allowing for increased metabolic resilience and energy efficiency.
