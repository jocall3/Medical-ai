# Mitochondrial Uncoupling for Metabolic Eradication

## Overview
Metabolic syndrome is driven by mitochondrial inefficiency and visceral fat accumulation.

## AI Logic
AI-designed mild mitochondrial uncouplers safely increase the proton leak in mitochondria. This forces the cell to dissipate excess energy as heat rather than storing it as fat, effectively eradicating metabolic syndrome and improving systemic redox balance.
