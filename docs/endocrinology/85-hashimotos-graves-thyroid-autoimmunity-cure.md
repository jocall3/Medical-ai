# Targeted Treg Engineering for Thyroid Autoimmunity

## Overview
Hashimoto's and Graves' diseases represent a breakdown in immune tolerance toward thyroid antigens.

## AI Logic
We deploy AI-engineered regulatory T-cells (Tregs) programmed to recognize thyroid-specific antigens. These cells migrate to the thyroid gland, suppressing local inflammation and halting the autoimmune destruction, effectively curing the disease by restoring self-tolerance.
