# Epigenetic Resensitization for Type 2 Diabetes

## Overview
Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) is driven by chronic insulin resistance, often exacerbated by epigenetic silencing of insulin signaling pathways. 

## AI Logic
Using high-throughput epigenetic profiling, the AI identifies specific DNA methylation patterns and histone deacetylase (HDAC) overexpression in peripheral tissues. Targeted epigenetic modifiers are then deployed to reverse these modifications, restoring insulin receptor sensitivity and effectively reversing metabolic syndrome at the transcriptional level.
