# HPO Axis Recalibration for PCOS

## Overview
Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a systemic disorder of the Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Ovarian (HPO) axis. 

## AI Logic
Building upon the ThyroidFeedbackEngine architecture, our AI models the HPO axis to identify the exact GnRH pulse frequency dysregulation. AI-designed peptides are then utilized to normalize the LH:FSH ratio, restoring natural ovulation and fertility without the side effects of traditional hormonal contraceptives.
