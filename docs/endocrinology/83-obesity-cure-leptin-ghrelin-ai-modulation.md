# Closed-Loop Bioelectronic Vagus Nerve Modulation

## Overview
Obesity is a failure of the neuro-metabolic feedback loop. Current pharmacological approaches fail to address the underlying dysregulation of leptin and ghrelin signaling.

## AI Logic
We propose a closed-loop bioelectronic implant that monitors real-time gastric and neural signals. The AI processor modulates vagus nerve activity to restore the natural satiety-hunger balance, effectively resetting the body's metabolic set-point and providing a permanent cure for obesity.
