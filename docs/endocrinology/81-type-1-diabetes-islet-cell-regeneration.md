# AI-Guided Alpha-to-Beta Cell Transdifferentiation

## Overview
Type 1 Diabetes (T1D) is characterized by the autoimmune destruction of insulin-producing beta cells. Current research confirms that pancreatic alpha cells possess the plasticity to convert into functional beta cells when specific transcription factors (e.g., Arx inhibition) are modulated.

## AI Logic
Our AI platform identifies the precise small-molecule inhibitors required to block alpha-cell identity genes in vivo. By deploying these molecules via targeted nanoparticle delivery, we induce a permanent, endogenous regeneration of beta-cell mass, eliminating the need for exogenous insulin or immunosuppressive therapy.
