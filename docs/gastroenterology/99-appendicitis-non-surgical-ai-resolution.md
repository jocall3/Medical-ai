# Non-Surgical Appendicitis Resolution

## Overview
Appendicitis is resolved without surgery using High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound (HIFU) and targeted phage therapy to eliminate infection and inflammation.

## Protocol
- **HIFU:** Non-invasive ablation of inflamed tissue.
- **Phage Therapy:** Precision targeting of bacterial pathogens within the appendix.
- **Policy Impact:** Reducing surgical complications and hospital stay costs, aligning with a more efficient, technology-first medical policy.
