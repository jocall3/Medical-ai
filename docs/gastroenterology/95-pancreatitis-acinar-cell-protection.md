# Predictive Pancreatitis Prevention

## Overview
AI-driven predictive modeling identifies the biochemical precursors to acute pancreatitis, allowing for the preemptive deployment of synthetic protease inhibitors.

## Technical Specs
- **Detection:** Continuous monitoring of serum amylase/lipase markers via subcutaneous biosensors.
- **Intervention:** Automated release of protease inhibitors to prevent acinar cell auto-digestion.
- **Policy Note:** Shifting from emergency room crisis management to predictive, automated health maintenance.
