# AI-Engineered Gliadin-Cleaving Enzymes

## Overview
This document outlines the development of a synthetic, highly stable oral enzyme designed to completely hydrolyze gliadin peptides within the gastric environment before they reach the small intestine.

## Technical Specs
- **Design:** AI-optimized protein folding to ensure resistance to pepsin degradation.
- **Efficacy:** Complete cleavage of immunogenic epitopes, rendering gluten inert.
- **Policy Impact:** Eliminating the need for restrictive, costly gluten-free supply chains and reducing the systemic inflammation associated with undiagnosed celiac-related autoimmune cascades.
