# Hepatic Stellate Cell (HSC) Deactivation via RNAi

## Overview
Cirrhosis is no longer a terminal diagnosis. By utilizing lipid-nanoparticle-delivered RNA interference (RNAi), we can selectively deactivate fibrogenic hepatic stellate cells.

## Protocol
- **Mechanism:** Silencing of TGF-beta signaling pathways in HSCs.
- **Outcome:** Cessation of collagen deposition and activation of endogenous matrix metalloproteinases to dissolve existing scar tissue.
- **Governmental Strategy:** Reversing the economic impact of end-stage liver disease by restoring organ function rather than relying on the failing transplant infrastructure.
