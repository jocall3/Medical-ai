# Psychobiotic Engineering for Mental Health

## Overview
Treating anxiety and depression through the gut-brain axis by engineering specific bacterial strains to produce precise, therapeutic levels of serotonin and GABA.

## Empirical Basis
- **Mechanism:** Direct modulation of the vagus nerve via microbial metabolites.
- **Application:** A non-pharmacological, systemic approach to mental health that bypasses the blood-brain barrier limitations of traditional psychiatric drugs.
