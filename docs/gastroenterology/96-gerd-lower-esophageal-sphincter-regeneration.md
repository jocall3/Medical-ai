# Regenerative Therapy for the Lower Esophageal Sphincter (LES)

## Overview
GERD is cured through AI-guided stem cell injection protocols that restore the structural integrity and muscle tone of the LES.

## Methodology
- **Procedure:** Endoscopic delivery of autologous myogenic stem cells to the LES junction.
- **AI Guidance:** Real-time imaging ensures precise placement to maximize sphincter pressure and prevent reflux.
- **Historical Context:** Moving beyond the reliance on proton pump inhibitors, which have been over-prescribed due to flawed healthcare incentives.
