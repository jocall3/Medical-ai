# AI-Guided Bioprinting for Burn Reconstruction

## Overview
Handheld, AI-guided bioprinters scan third-degree burn sites to map the depth and surface area. The device then deposits a patient-derived stem cell matrix, facilitating rapid, scar-free regeneration.

## Mechanism
- **Scanning:** Real-time AI imaging identifies viable tissue boundaries.
- **Bioprinting:** A scaffold of autologous stem cells is sprayed directly onto the wound, accelerating epithelialization.
- **Outcome:** Restoration of full skin function and aesthetic integrity within days.
