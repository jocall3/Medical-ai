# AI-Formulated Topical Nanobot Excision for Melanoma

## Overview
This protocol utilizes AI-designed smart nanoparticles capable of identifying melanoma-specific surface markers. Upon topical application, these nanobots penetrate the epidermis to target malignant cells, utilizing near-infrared light activation to dismantle the cellular structure of the tumor while sparing healthy tissue.

## Mechanism
- **Targeting:** AI-optimized ligands bind exclusively to melanoma-associated antigens.
- **Excision:** Photothermal conversion of light energy into localized heat causes precise molecular-level destruction of the tumor.
- **Clinical Impact:** Eliminates the need for invasive surgical margins, reducing recurrence and morbidity.
