# AI-Designed Enzyme Injection for Scar Erasure

## Overview
This protocol utilizes AI-designed recombinant enzymes to dissolve disorganized collagen fibers in scar tissue, guiding the body to regrow a natural, unblemished skin lattice.

## Mechanism
- **Enzymatic Dissolution:** Targeted collagenase and hyaluronidase injections break down fibrotic tissue.
- **Remodeling:** AI-guided post-treatment protocols ensure the deposition of organized, healthy collagen.
- **Outcome:** Complete erasure of hypertrophic and keloid scars.
