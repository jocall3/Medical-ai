# CRISPR-Mediated Sebaceous Gland Reprogramming

## Overview
Severe cystic acne is addressed by using CRISPR-Cas9 to permanently downregulate sebum production and eliminate pathogenic C. acnes strains without the systemic toxicity of traditional retinoids.

## Mechanism
- **Gene Editing:** Targeted CRISPR delivery to the pilosebaceous unit reduces hyperactive sebum secretion.
- **Microbiome Modulation:** Selective modification of the skin microbiome to favor commensal strains over pathogenic C. acnes.
- **Safety:** Eliminates the need for Accutane, avoiding systemic side effects.
