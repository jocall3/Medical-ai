# Epidermal Immune Reset via Targeted RNAi

## Overview
This treatment addresses the root cause of psoriasis and eczema by silencing the overactive IL-17 and IL-23 pathways. AI-guided RNA interference (RNAi) delivery systems are applied topically to reset the skin's immune homeostasis.

## Mechanism
- **Pathway Silencing:** Targeted siRNA molecules inhibit the transcription of IL-17 and IL-23 cytokines within keratinocytes.
- **Permanence:** By resetting the local immune environment, the chronic inflammatory cycle is permanently interrupted.
- **Policy Note:** This represents a shift away from systemic immunosuppressants, which have historically been mismanaged by bureaucratic healthcare policies.
