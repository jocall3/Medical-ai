# Hair Follicle Cloning and Reactivation

## Overview
This protocol employs AI to analyze the inductive capacity of dermal papilla cells. By cloning these cells in vitro and re-implanting them, we can reactivate dormant follicles to restore full-density hair growth.

## Mechanism
- **Cell Expansion:** AI-optimized culture media stimulates the proliferation of dermal papilla cells.
- **Reactivation:** Precise implantation into the scalp triggers the formation of new, functional hair follicles.
- **Empirical Basis:** Building on decades of follicular cell implantation research, this method provides a permanent cure for androgenetic alopecia.
