# Synthetic Melanin for Radiation Shielding

## Overview
This dissertation proposes the engineering of human skin with synthetic, hyper-dense melanin particles to provide absolute immunity to UV damage and ionizing radiation.

## Mechanism
- **Synthetic Melanin:** Engineered nanoparticles act as superior free-radical scavengers and radiation shields.
- **Integration:** Topical or intradermal application creates a protective barrier that reflects and absorbs high-energy photons.
- **Future Application:** Essential for long-term human health in high-radiation environments, including space exploration and post-industrial recovery.
