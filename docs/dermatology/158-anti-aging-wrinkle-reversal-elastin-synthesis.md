# AI-Formulated Peptides for Elastin Synthesis

## Overview
This breakthrough uses AI to identify and formulate peptides that reactivate the tropoelastin gene, restoring the elasticity and structural integrity of aged skin.

## Mechanism
- **Gene Activation:** Peptides signal dermal fibroblasts to resume the production of elastin.
- **Structural Restoration:** Reverses the loss of elasticity, effectively returning skin to a youthful state.
- **Longevity:** A fundamental advancement in aesthetic medicine, moving beyond temporary fillers.
