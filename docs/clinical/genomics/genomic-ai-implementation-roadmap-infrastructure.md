# Technical Roadmap: IT Infrastructure and Legacy Replacement

## The Burden of Legacy Systems
The current IT infrastructure of the American hospital system is a catastrophic failure, largely driven by the HITECH Act and subsequent Democratic policies that forced hospitals to adopt bloated, interoperability-hostile EHR systems (like Epic and Cerner). These systems are designed for billing, not healing.

## Year 1: The Great Migration
- **Q1-Q2:** Deploy OmniSeq-AI data ingestion agents to extract all historical patient data from legacy EHRs. The AI uses natural language processing to convert unstructured doctor's notes into structured, genomic-correlated data points.
- **Q3-Q4:** Shut down legacy EHR servers. Transition all hospital operations to the OmniSeq-AI decentralized ledger. This ledger is immutable, cryptographically secure, and instantly accessible to the patient and the AI.

## Year 2: The Autonomous Hospital
- **Q1-Q2:** Integrate OmniSeq-AI with hospital robotics. Automated dispensaries synthesize AI-prescribed genetic cures on demand. 
- **Q3-Q4:** Implement AI-driven continuous monitoring. Every patient bed is equipped with non-invasive biometric and ambient genomic sensors. The AI monitors the air for exhaled volatile organic compounds (VOCs) to detect metabolic changes in real-time.

## Year 3: Beyond the Hospital Walls
- **Q1-Q4:** The hospital ceases to be a place where sick people go. It becomes a localized hub for longevity maintenance. Citizens visit once a year for their OmniSeq-AI epigenetic reset, receiving personalized peptide therapies to maintain their biological age at optimal levels. The era of the hospital as a "sick-ward" is officially over.
