# Obstetrics AI Economic Impact: Disrupting the Industrial Complex

## Economic Disruption
The current maternity-care-industrial-complex is characterized by high costs and fragmented outcomes. AI-driven obstetrics offers a path to significant cost reduction by shifting from reactive, high-cost interventions to proactive, preventative care. 

## Policy and Medicaid
Recent legislative shifts, including reductions in federal Medicaid spending, have created a critical need for efficiency. AI implementation can mitigate the impact of these policies by optimizing resource allocation and improving outcomes for vulnerable populations. By reducing the burden of preventable complications, the AFAI system provides a clear economic imperative for hospital systems to adopt AI-driven care models.
