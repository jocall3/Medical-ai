# Obstetrics AI Workflows: Clinical Integration

## Continuous Monitoring
AI-driven obstetrics shifts the paradigm from episodic check-ups to continuous, non-invasive monitoring. The AFAI system integrates with existing hospital infrastructure to provide real-time alerts for amniotic fluid status, enabling clinicians to intervene before critical thresholds are reached.

## Predictive Triage
Workflows are designed to augment, not replace, human expertise. The system provides automated triage, flagging high-risk pregnancies for immediate specialist review. By automating routine segmentation and volume calculation, the AI reduces administrative burden, allowing obstetricians to focus on complex patient interactions and personalized care plans.
