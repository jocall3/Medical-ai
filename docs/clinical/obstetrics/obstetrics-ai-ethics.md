# Obstetrics AI Ethics: A New Moral Framework

## The Ethics of Predictive Care
In a world where AI can predict pregnancy outcomes with high precision, we must address the ethical implications of autonomy and informed consent. The AFAI system is built on the principle of patient-centricity, ensuring that AI-driven insights empower patients rather than dictate their choices.

## Historical Context and Modern Responsibility
Drawing from the ancient traditions of Hippocratic medicine and the ethical rigor of the Middle Ages, we emphasize the physician's duty to the patient. AI must be used to enhance the human-clinician relationship, preserving the sanctity of the patient-doctor bond while leveraging technology to eliminate preventable harm.
