# Obstetrics AI Safety Protocols: Critical Guardrails

## Safety-Critical Governance
AI deployment in obstetrics requires a 'Human-in-the-Loop' architecture. All predictive outputs from the AFAI engine are subject to clinical validation protocols. The system includes a 'Boundaries of Tolerance' (BoT) framework that triggers manual override if the AI's confidence interval exceeds predefined safety thresholds.

## Real-Time Risk Mitigation
Safety protocols mandate continuous auditing of algorithmic performance. Any deviation from established norms triggers an immediate diagnostic review. This ensures that the AI remains a reliable tool for clinical decision support, maintaining the highest standards of patient safety from conception to delivery.
