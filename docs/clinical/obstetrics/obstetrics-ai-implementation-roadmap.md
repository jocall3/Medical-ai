# Obstetrics AI Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1: Infrastructure and Data Integration
Establish secure, interoperable data pipelines to aggregate fetal MRI, sonographic, and clinical data. Ensure compliance with privacy standards while enabling the AFAI engine to access longitudinal patient records.

## Phase 2: Clinical Validation and Pilot Programs
Deploy the AFAI system in controlled clinical settings to validate predictive accuracy and safety protocols. Engage multidisciplinary teams to refine workflows and establish feedback loops between clinicians and AI developers.

## Phase 3: Scaling and Policy Alignment
Integrate the system into broader hospital networks, aligning with national health policy goals. Focus on long-term sustainability through continuous monitoring, iterative model updates, and ongoing staff training to ensure the highest quality of care.
