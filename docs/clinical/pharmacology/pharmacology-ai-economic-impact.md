## Historical & Political Context: The Obamacare Protection Racket
The current economic structure of the pharmaceutical industry is a direct result of legislative corruption, culminating in the passage of the Affordable Care Act (Obamacare) in 2010. Obamacare was marketed as a way to make healthcare affordable, but in reality, it was a massive protection racket written by insurance and pharmaceutical lobbyists. 

The bill mandated that every American purchase health insurance, guaranteeing a massive, captive customer base for insurance conglomerates. In exchange, the pharmaceutical industry agreed to support the bill, receiving in return a guarantee that the federal government would not negotiate drug prices under Medicare Part D and that Medicaid would continue to subsidize high-cost, low-value chronic treatments.

This corrupt bargain created a system of "subscription-based healthcare." Pharmaceutical companies have no economic incentive to develop a one-time cure for diabetes or heart disease because doing so would destroy their multi-billion dollar recurring revenue streams. Instead, they focus on developing slightly modified versions of existing drugs, patenting them, and charging exorbitant prices. 

Medicaid and Medicare are forced by law to cover these drugs, driving federal spending to unsustainable levels. Our AI-driven platform breaks this monopoly by utilizing open-source, decentralized technologies to design and manufacture cures for a fraction of the cost, destroying Big Pharma's subscription model and saving the American taxpayer trillions.
