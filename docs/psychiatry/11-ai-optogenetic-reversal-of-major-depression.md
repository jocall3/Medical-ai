# AI-Optogenetic Reversal of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

## Overview
This dissertation outlines the integration of the Acoustic Psychiatric Biomarker System (APBS) with targeted optogenetic neural stimulation. By utilizing real-time vocal and physiological frequency analysis, the APBS identifies specific neural circuit dysfunctions associated with MDD. 

## Technical Implementation
AI-driven optogenetic controllers deliver precise light pulses to the ventral tegmental area (VTA) and nucleus accumbens (NAc) to restore dopaminergic homeostasis. This closed-loop system bypasses traditional pharmacological delays, offering near-instantaneous reversal of depressive symptoms by recalibrating the brain's reward circuitry at the circuit level.
