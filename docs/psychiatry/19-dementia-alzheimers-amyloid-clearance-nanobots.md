# Dementia/Alzheimer's: Amyloid Clearance Nanobots

## Implementation
C++ AVX2 SIMD instructions are used to control a swarm of nanobots. These bots are engineered to cross the blood-brain barrier and identify amyloid-beta plaques.

## Mechanism
Once identified, the nanobots phagocytize the plaques, clearing the neural pathways and restoring cognitive function. This represents a definitive cure for neurodegenerative plaque-based dementia.
