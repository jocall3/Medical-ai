# Schizophrenia: Neural Circuit Correction via AI

## Methodology
We utilize the `ActionPotentialSimulator.ts` library to model aberrant neural firing patterns characteristic of schizophrenic hallucinations. 

## BCI Deployment
Closed-loop Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCI) are deployed to detect these specific firing signatures in real-time. The BCI emits counter-phase neural signals to cancel out the aberrant activity, effectively silencing hallucinations and restoring cognitive coherence.
