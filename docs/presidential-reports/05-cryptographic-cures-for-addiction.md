# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS: CRYPTOGRAPHIC NEURO-INTERCEPTION FOR ADDICTION

**SUBJECT:** Eradication of Chemical Dependency via Biometric Telemetry
**TECHNOLOGY:** Homomorphic Encryption & Neurochemical Vector Prediction

## 1. THE PROBLEM: THE RELAPSE VECTOR
Addiction is a failure of the prefrontal cortex to override the dopaminergic reward system. Current treatments fail because they are reactive, not predictive.

## 2. THE SOLUTION: CONTINUOUS BIOMETRIC TELEMETRY
We implement a subcutaneous neural-link array that streams real-time neurochemical data to the AI middleware.

### 2.1 Homomorphic Encryption for Privacy
To maintain absolute privacy and security, the data is processed using **Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)**. The AI can analyze the neurochemical patterns and predict a relapse *without ever decrypting the raw data*. The computation happens on the encrypted ciphertext.

### 2.2 The Interception Protocol
1. **Pattern Recognition:** The AI identifies the 'Relapse Signature'—a specific sequence of cortisol spikes and dopamine dips that precede a craving by 15 minutes.
2. **Predictive Trigger:** The AI triggers a localized, micro-dose release of an antagonist (e.g., a targeted naltrexone derivative) from an implanted reservoir.
3. **Neurochemical Neutralization:** The craving is neutralized before the patient is even aware of it. The 'urge' is mathematically deleted from the brain's current state.
