# Microscopic Inner-Ear Vestibular Implants with Active AI Noise Cancellation

## 1. Executive Summary
Vertigo and Meniere's disease are debilitating vestibular disorders characterized by severe, unpredictable episodes of spinning, nausea, and cognitive disorientation. This paper details a microscopic inner-ear implant that acts as an active noise-canceling system for the vestibular system. By integrating a micro-gyroscope with a closed-loop AI processor, the implant detects aberrant vestibular nerve signals and delivers real-time, neutralizing electrical stimulation, instantly curing vertigo and restoring perfect balance.

## 2. The Science of Vestibular Neuromodulation
The vestibular system relies on the precise movement of endolymph fluid within the semicircular canals to stimulate hair cells and signal head rotation. In Meniere's disease, endolymphatic hydrops (fluid buildup) causes mechanical distortion, leading to erratic, high-frequency firing of the vestibular nerve that the brain interprets as violent spinning.

Our microscopic implant is placed within the mastoid cavity, with microelectrode leads inserted into the superior, horizontal, and posterior semicircular canals. The implant contains an ultra-precise 6-axis micro-electromechanical system (MEMS) gyroscope that measures actual physical head movement.
