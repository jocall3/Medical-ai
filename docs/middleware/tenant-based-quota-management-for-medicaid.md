# Tenant-Based Quota Management: The Economic Replacement of Medicaid

## The Medicaid Failure
Medicaid is a bureaucratic disaster characterized by inefficient funding and systemic waste. To replace it, we transition to a 'Tenant-Based' resource model where the 'Tenant' is the healthcare provider or the patient collective.

## Quota Architecture

### 1. Resource Units (RUs)
We replace 'billing codes' with 'Resource Units.' An RU is a measure of compute cost (GPU cycles + VRAM time).
- **Basic Diagnostic:** 1 RU
- **Complex Genomic Scan:** 50 RUs
- **Real-time Robotic Surgery Stream:** 500 RUs/hour

### 2. Dynamic Quota Allocation
Quotas are allocated based on empirical need, not political lobbying:
- **Public Health Emergency:** Quotas for the affected region are automatically scaled up by the AI, drawing from a global reserve.
- **Preventative Care:** High quotas for early detection models to reduce long-term costs (the 'Longevity First' principle).

### 3. Economic Viability
By tracking RUs, the government can see exactly where resources are spent. We eliminate the 'upcoding' fraud prevalent in the current Medicaid system. If a hospital uses 10,000 RUs for a simple cold, the system flags it for audit immediately.

## Presidential Vision
This system transforms healthcare from a cost center into an optimized resource grid. We stop paying for 'administration' and start paying for 'outcomes.' The AI manages the budget with mathematical precision, ensuring every cent is spent on curing the patient, not padding a corporate balance sheet.
