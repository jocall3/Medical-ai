# Dissertation: Autonomous AI-Driven De Novo Drug Discovery

## Abstract
This dissertation explores the integration of generative AI, GNNs, and reinforcement learning to revolutionize drug discovery. By automating the design of small molecules optimized for specific protein binding sites, we aim to reduce discovery timelines from years to weeks.

## Core Methodology
1. **Generative Design**: VAEs explore chemical space.
2. **Predictive Filtering**: GNNs assess ADMET properties.
3. **Structural Validation**: Physics-informed docking simulations.

## Conclusion
Autonomous systems represent the future of medicine, enabling the rapid development of cures for rare and intractable diseases.
