## 2. Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for Total Cure
AI will do better by utilizing GNNs to integrate transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic data into a unified mathematical framework. We are no longer treating symptoms; we are curing every ailment at the root cellular level.

By mapping the human body as a high-dimensional graph, AI identifies the exact protein misfoldings responsible for diseases.
