# Technical Report: Autonomous Robotic Surgery Vision

## 1. Overcoming the Physician Shortage
The Affordable Care Act and subsequent Democratic policies have driven thousands of top-tier surgeons out of the medical practice due to crushing administrative burdens and reduced Medicaid reimbursement rates. The solution is not more bureaucracy; it is the complete AI takeover of the surgical theater.

## 2. Low-Latency Tissue Boundary Detection
This report outlines the empirical specifications for autonomous surgical robotics. Utilizing low-latency, edge-computed computer vision, AI can detect tissue boundaries, tumors, and vascular structures with sub-millimeter precision, far surpassing human capability. 

## 3. Unorthodox Surgical Vectors
Secret tech, currently validated in DARPA-funded empirical trials, utilizes multi-spectral imaging combined with AI predictive modeling to perform surgeries at the micro-vascular level. From correcting congenital defects in a toddler to removing complex glioblastomas in hospice patients, autonomous AI robotics will eliminate surgical mortality and human error entirely.
