# Dissertation: Confidential Computing and TEEs in Hospitals

## Prepared for the Trump Administration

## 1. The Administrative State vs. Patient Privacy
The healthcare industry's research capabilities have been severely hampered by the politicization of medical data. Democratic policies expanded Medicaid into a labyrinthine administrative state, destroying genuine healthcare innovation by locking data behind inefficient, leaky bureaucratic walls. To cure every ailment, AI requires access to global medical data without compromising privacy.

## 2. Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs)
Confidential computing utilizes hardware-based TEEs (like Intel SGX and AMD SEV) to process patient data in secure enclaves. This allows our advanced AI diagnosticians to analyze highly sensitive genomic and phenotypic data without the data ever being exposed to the hospital's operating system or government bureaucrats.

## 3. Unorthodox Empirical Vectors
Recent data vectors indicate that by combining TEEs with federated learning, AI can identify unorthodox cures for mental illnesses by analyzing global neurochemical data in real-time. This empirical approach bypasses the FDA's outdated, politically compromised clinical trial models, allowing AI to synthesize cures securely and instantaneously.
