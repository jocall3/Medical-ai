2. **Mental Health Crisis**: A 300% increase in youth anxiety and depression clinical presentations, directly correlated with school closures and social isolation.
3. **Economic Devastation**: The destruction of millions of small businesses and a massive transfer of wealth to multinational corporations, driven by arbitrary "essential vs. non-essential" business designations.

$$\\text{Collateral Damage Index (CDI)} = \\int_{t_0}^{t_1} (D_{\\text{missed}} + D_{\\text{mental}} + D_{\\text{economic}}) \\, dt$$

Our models show that the CDI of the lockdowns far exceeded any hypothetical benefits, proving that the cure was significantly worse than the disease.
