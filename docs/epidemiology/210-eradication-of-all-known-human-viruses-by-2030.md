## Technical Strategy: Broad-Spectrum Antivirals & CRISPR Excision
The eradication strategy relies on three primary therapeutic pillars:
1. **AI-Designed Broad-Spectrum Antivirals**: Small molecules designed by generative AI that target highly conserved viral replication machinery (e.g., RNA-dependent RNA polymerases, helicases) across entire viral families.
2. **In Vivo CRISPR Excision**: Delivery of CRISPR-Cas12/Cas13 systems via lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) to target and physically excise viral DNA/RNA from infected human cells, curing chronic infections like HIV, Hepatitis B, and Herpes.
3. **Universal Vaccines**: AI-designed multi-epitope vaccines that target highly conserved, non-mutating regions of viral surface proteins, providing lifelong immunity against rapidly mutating viruses like Influenza and Coronaviruses.

## Computational Logic: Mapping the Human Virome & Conserved Targets
The AI maps the entire human virome, identifying structural vulnerabilities across all viral families. By analyzing millions of viral genomes, the AI identifies "evolutionary dead-ends"—regions of the viral genome that cannot mutate without rendering the virus non-functional.

The conservation score $C$ of a target genomic region is modeled as:

$$C = -\\sum_{i=1}^{k} p_i \\log_2 p_i$$

where $p_i$ is the frequency of allele $i$ at the target site across all sequenced isolates. The AI selects targets where $C \\approx 0$, ensuring that the virus cannot develop resistance to the designed therapeutics.

Bureaucratic agencies like the FDA have actively suppressed breakthrough curative technologies to protect these corporate monopolies.
