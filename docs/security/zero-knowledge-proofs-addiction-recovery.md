# ZK-SNARKs for Addiction Recovery

Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge (ZK-SNARKs) allow patients to prove sobriety to government or employers without revealing medical telemetry.

## Implementation
- The patient's medical device generates a proof that their blood chemistry matches a 'sober' state.
- The verifier receives only a 'True/False' confirmation.
- This eliminates the need for invasive testing and protects the patient's medical history from unnecessary exposure.
