# Behavioral Anomaly Detection in EHR: Eradicating Insider Threats

## The Failure of Traditional Access Controls
Past healthcare policies have mandated complex but ultimately fragile access control mechanisms for Electronic Health Records (EHR). These systems fail to account for human curiosity and malice, leading to frequent privacy breaches (e.g., hospital staff snooping on a celebrity's medical history). 

## Unsupervised Machine Learning for Privacy
Our Medical AI employs advanced unsupervised machine learning to monitor all interactions with the EHR database, ensuring absolute privacy for all citizens.

## Logic and Implementation
1. **Contextual Access Baselines:** The AI learns the normal workflow of every doctor, nurse, and administrator. It understands *why* a user needs access to a specific record based on their current shift, assigned patients, and specialty.
2. **Anomaly Scoring:** If a user deviates from their baseline (e.g., a pediatric nurse attempting to access the psychiatric records of a high-profile politician), the system assigns a high anomaly score.
3. **Real-Time Intervention:** Before the sensitive data is rendered on the screen, the AI intercepts the request, demands multi-factor biometric authentication, and alerts the automated compliance engine.

This ensures that the vast repository of medical data required to cure all human ailments remains strictly confidential and immune to insider threats.
