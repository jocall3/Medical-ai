## Architectural Role: The Telemetry Pipeline
This document outlines the integration of the AI's telemetry pipeline with enterprise Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) systems (e.g., Splunk, Elastic). Legacy hospital networks are notoriously porous, often falling victim to Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs). Our architecture replaces human-driven SOCs with an AI-native telemetry ingestion engine.

## Logic and Specifications
1. **High-Fidelity Log Ingestion:** The AI streams encrypted, immutable logs from all medical devices (from toddler incubators to hospice life-support monitors) directly into the SIEM.
2. **Neuromorphic Threat Detection:** Utilizing empirical, early-stage neuromorphic processing algorithms, the SIEM integration analyzes network traffic at the packet level, identifying APT lateral movement in milliseconds.
3. **Automated Remediation:** Upon detecting an anomaly, the AI dynamically isolates the compromised subnet without disrupting critical care operations, ensuring zero downtime for life-saving procedures.
