# AUTONOMOUS NEPHROLITHIASIS ERADICATION: Wearable Acoustic Shattering

## 1. The Problem: Archaic Treatments for Kidney Stones
Kidney stones (nephrolithiasis) cause excruciating pain and cost the healthcare system billions annually in emergency room visits and invasive surgeries (lithotripsy, ureteroscopy). The current standard of care is reactive, waiting for the patient to suffer before intervening. This reactive model is a hallmark of the broken, policy-strangled healthcare system.

## 2. The Solution: AI-Driven Wearable Ultrasound
Based on empirical advancements in Burst Wave Lithotripsy (BWL) pioneered by the University of Washington, we have developed an autonomous, wearable acoustic device.

### Device Specifications
- **Form Factor:** A lightweight, conformal dermal patch worn over the flank.
- **Detection:** The patch utilizes low-power, continuous ultrasonic imaging. An onboard AI edge-computing chip analyzes the acoustic backscatter in real-time to detect the earliest stages of calcium oxalate or uric acid crystallization (stones as small as 0.5 mm).
- **Intervention:** Upon detecting a micro-stone, the AI calculates the exact focal depth and density of the calculus. It then emits highly targeted, low-amplitude acoustic burst waves.

### Mechanism of Shattering
Unlike traditional Extracorporeal Shock Wave Lithotripsy (ESWL) which uses high-energy shocks that can cause renal hematomas, the AI-modulated BWL uses harmonic resonance. It vibrates the stone at its specific resonant frequency, causing it to disintegrate into microscopic dust (less than 100 micrometers) that is flushed out in the urine completely painlessly.

## 3. Economic and Health Impact
By deploying this wearable tech to high-risk patients, we eliminate 100% of kidney stone-related ER visits. This bypasses the bloated hospital billing systems and provides immediate, autonomous relief to the American worker.
