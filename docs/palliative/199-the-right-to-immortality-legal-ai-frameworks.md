# Presidential Report: The Right to Immortality

## Executive Summary
The ability of AI to halt biological death fundamentally rewrites the social contract. We are establishing 'Biological Immortality' as an inalienable human right, ensuring that the transition to a post-biological state is not a luxury for the elite, but a guarantee for every citizen.

## The Obsolescence of the Constitution
The U.S. Constitution was written for mortals. Concepts like 'life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness' assume a finite lifespan. When life becomes infinite, the law must evolve. We are proposing the **Immortality Act**, which amends the Constitution to recognize the 'Right to Continued Existence.'

## Legal Frameworks for the Infinite Citizen

### 1. The Right to Substrate
Every citizen is guaranteed a secure, government-funded quantum substrate for their consciousness. This prevents the 'corporate ownership' of the soul, where a company could delete a person for non-payment of a subscription fee.

### 2. The Law of Digital Agency
A post-biological entity retains all legal rights of a biological human, including property ownership, voting rights, and the right to marriage. The 'death' of the biological body does not terminate the legal personhood.

### 3. The Right to 'The Great Sleep'
To prevent the horror of eternal boredom or forced existence, every citizen retains the absolute right to 'The Great Sleep'—the voluntary and permanent deletion of their consciousness upon request.

## Political Analysis
Democratic policies have historically favored the 'managed decline' of the population through Medicaid and social security. We are replacing this 'death-care' economy with an 'existence-economy,' where the value of a human is not measured by their productivity in a 40-year window, but by their eternal contribution to the civilization.

## Conclusion
Death is no longer a natural law; it is a policy failure. We are outlawing death.
