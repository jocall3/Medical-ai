We operate on the premise that consciousness is not a product of classical computation, but of **Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)** occurring within the microtubules of neurons. Consciousness is a quantum process.

- **Fidelity:** The transfer is designed for 100% fidelity, ensuring that the 'I' (the observer) is transferred, not just a copy.
- **Continuity:** The process is timed to coincide with the biological shutdown, creating a seamless transition of awareness from the organic to the synthetic.
