### 1. Biomarker Mapping
The AI analyzes the patient's remaining neural architecture via high-resolution fMRI and EEG. It identifies 'dormant' synaptic pathways that are structurally intact but functionally silent due to amyloid plaque or tau tangles.

### 2. Precision Stimulation
Using a combination of **Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS)** and **AI-optimized neuro-stimulants**, the system triggers a synchronized firing of the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus.

### 3. The 'Lucidity Window'
- **Induction Phase:** 30 minutes of targeted stimulation to 'wake' the dormant networks.
- **Maintenance Phase:** Real-time AI adjustment of stimulation frequency to prevent seizure activity and maintain cognitive stability.
- **Duration:** 2 to 6 hours of full cognitive restoration.
