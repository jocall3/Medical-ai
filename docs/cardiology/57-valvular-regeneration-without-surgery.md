# Presidential Report: In Vivo Valvular Regeneration via Bioelectric Fields

## Executive Summary
Open-heart surgery for valve replacement is a traumatic event with high morbidity. We propose the **Bioelectric Valve Regeneration System**, which uses targeted electric fields to stimulate the body's own stem cells to rebuild the mitral and aortic valves in vivo.

## The Bioelectric Logic

### Endogenous Field Manipulation
Research has shown that arteries and valves possess inherent ferroelectric properties. The AI-controlled system applies a precise, low-frequency electromagnetic field to the valve leaflets.
- **Homing**: The field is tuned to attract circulating mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) to the damaged valve tissue.
- **Differentiation**: The AI modulates the field's frequency to trigger the differentiation of these stem cells into valvular interstitial cells (VICs), which then secrete the necessary collagen and elastin to rebuild the valve's structure.

### Non-Invasive Delivery
The system is delivered via a temporary, catheter-based electrode array that is removed once the valve has reached the required thickness and elasticity, as verified by AI-driven echocardiography.

## The Medicaid Research Gap
Government funding has been funneled into 'replacement' (mechanical valves) rather than 'regeneration.' This is a failure of the current research grants system, which favors established, safe-bet technologies over disruptive cures.

## Empirical Evidence
- **Ferroelectric Arteries**: Based on research showing that the aorta reacts to external electric fields.
- **Stem Cell Homing**: A proven biological phenomenon where cells migrate toward specific chemical or electrical gradients.
